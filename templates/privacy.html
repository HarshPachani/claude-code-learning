Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 10, 2026

1. Data We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, including your email address for account registration and any financial transactions (amount, category, date, and description) that you choose to log in the application. We also collect basic technical information such as IP addresses and browser types to improve the service.

2. How We Use Your Data

The data we collect is used exclusively to:

  • Provide and maintain the expense tracking service.
  • Generate spending patterns and financial summaries.
  • Manage your user account and authenticate your access.
  • Improve the app's performance and user experience.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely on our servers. We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. You may request the deletion of your account and associated data at any time.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personally identifiable or financial information to outside parties. We may use third-party infrastructure providers (such as cloud hosting services) that process data on our behalf under strict confidentiality agreements.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or the handling of your data, please contact us at privacy@spendly.example.com.
